Proposed action for the Focus Group
===================================

#. **Consider this lifecycle and its evidence criteria as an input to A.2.2.** The five stages are offered as a candidate answer to "conditions and evidence criteria required at each lifecycle stage", not as a settled one.
#. **Test the two-axis vocabulary against the group's own use cases.** The specific testable claim is that any single-status representation of trust loses at least one of the three legitimate states in §4.3. If a use case can be found in which a single scalar is sufficient, that would be a useful counterexample and this note would be revised.
#. **Keep the evidence criteria separable from the architecture.** The criteria are about what a relying party needs to establish; the architecture is about how it is delivered. Specifying them together tends to produce a framework that can only be implemented one way.
#. **Consider the retirement stage explicitly.** It is the stage most often omitted and the one where an unclosed record can still be relied upon.
